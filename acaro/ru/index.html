Frequently Asked Questions
(FAQ)

What ingredients are in the
pendant?

Our pendant contains a
synergistic blend of Camphor Oil, Mulberry Oil, Citronella Oil, Lavender Oil, Lemon Oil, and Peppermint Oil.

Does the pendant need to be
worn continuously?

No, the pendant must be worn only when you are outside or you want your dog protected. The active ingredients will be distributed through the air.

From what age can the
pendant be used?

Our pendant is recommended for puppies and kittens from the eighth week.

Is it safe for my pet? Does it
have any side effects?

Yes, it’s completely safe. The pendant uses only 100% natural essential oils—no harsh chemicals or pesticides. It's designed to be gentle on your pet while effectively repelling fleas, ticks, and other insects. As with any new product, we recommend monitoring your pet for the first few days. If you notice any signs of irritation, simply remove the pendant and consult your vet.

Will it still work if it gets wet?

Absolutely. The pendant is waterproof and weather-resistant, so it won’t lose effectiveness if your dog goes for a swim, plays in the rain, or rolls around in the mud. It's built to keep working in all kinds of outdoor conditions.

Will it irritate my pet’s skin?

The pendant is made to be worn on your pet's collar—not directly on the skin—and contains natural oils that are typically well tolerated. However, in rare cases, pets with sensitive skin may experience mild irritation. If you see any redness or itching, it’s best to remove the pendant and check with your veterinarian.
